Why Plum Exists

I built Plum because I needed accurate transcripts of my own meetings.

Modern speech recognition can produce highly accurate transcripts and distinguish speakers from a single recording. Yet I could not find a meeting app that did this reliably enough. Most focused on adding features around the transcript instead.

Plum focuses on the transcript itself.

What Plum Does

  • High-accuracy transcription. Plum uses the most reliable speech-to-text engine available to it. Today, that is ElevenLabs. As better technology becomes available, Plum will use it.
  • Speaker separation. Each speaker is identified and kept separate throughout the transcript.
  • Works with ordinary microphones. You should not need dedicated recording hardware to get a useful transcript. Plum uses modern speech recognition to get as much as possible from the recording you already have.
  • Completed transcripts. Plum processes the recording after the meeting instead of providing real-time transcription.
  • Open source. Plum's source code is public. View the source ↗
